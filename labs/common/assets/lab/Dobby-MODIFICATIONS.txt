Local research project modifications, 2026-09-09:
- external/logging/logging/logging.h: make the header-defined logger and its
  Shared accessor inline C++ entities to avoid duplicate definitions.
- source/InterceptEntry.cpp: explicitly initialize thumb_mode to false before
  checking whether an ARM32 function address selects Thumb mode. The original
  constructor left this flag uninitialized for ARM-mode targets.

The reproducible modifications are implemented by tools/setup_hook.py in the
research source. They are not an unmodified upstream Dobby release.
